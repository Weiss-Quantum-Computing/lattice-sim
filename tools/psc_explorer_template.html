AFP transfer per level

Probability that each pulse of the selected loop makes its intended transition, against the Franck–Condon factor. A transfer near 1 means the pulse is saturated; it drops only near a node, outside the sweep, or where the carrier coupling shifts the line.

Outcome of one step per level

Clean: n → n−1 with no optical pumping. Pumped: ends in F = 3 and is pumped (≈2.1 photons, heats all axes). Stuck: nothing happens. Levels where clean drops are where the step relies on optical pumping.
